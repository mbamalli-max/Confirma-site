About Konfirmata

Tamper-evident business activity record infrastructure for thin-file, underbanked, and economically active populations

Konfirmata’s mission is to turn daily economic activity into verifiable business records that underserved entrepreneurs can use when a lender, program, or institution asks for proof.

Mission

Make invisible business activity visible in a form institutions can trust.

Konfirmata is designed for entrepreneurs whose businesses are active, valuable, and persistent, but whose business histories are still hard for formal systems to evaluate. The infrastructure is designed to be accessible where conventional documentation is absent or incomplete.

Konfirmata does not independently verify that an underlying transaction occurred. It produces tamper-evident business activity records whose integrity, sequence, and device origin can be cryptographically verified.

The aim is practical rather than abstract: help entrepreneurs create durable records they own and control, help institutions review them more fairly when entrepreneurs choose to share them, and reduce the gap between real economic activity and conventional documentation.

Mission in one line

Konfirmata is not bookkeeping software. It is infrastructure for building business records that institutions can actually rely on.

Patent

Protected architecture, practical mission.

Konfirmata’s core technology is the subject of USPTO Provisional Patent Application No. 63/987,858, filed February 2026. The filing covers methods and systems for creating verifiable, tamper-evident business records that can support institutional review.

Verification infrastructure deployed. Public attestation endpoint available at konfirmata.com/verify. If you represent an institution or community program and want to discuss record review, get in touch.
