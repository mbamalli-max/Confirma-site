Methodology

External accounts

External accounts are all app accounts except those the owner has excluded as internal test, demo or administrator accounts. Payment, records or activity are not required. Exclusion is the owner's own entry; it is not an independent confirmation, and the time of the last change is not a review of every account.

Timing

Records are counted by the time the server received them, not the time recorded on the phone. Activity on a phone that has not synced is not seen until it syncs. The current week or month in a chart is in progress and not complete.

Audience figures

Country, sector and business type describe the current profiles of External accounts. Business type categories too small to show safely are withheld or combined as "Other". Countries and sectors are shown with their exact counts, however small. Withheld figures are never shown as zero.

Business sectors

Sectors are Konfirmata's own categories for organising businesses; they are not an official industry classification or a certification. Every business profile is in exactly one sector, chosen by its main activity. For a business type in the catalog the sector comes from the catalog. For a business that is not listed it is the sector the account holder chose, or "Other business" when no sector could be determined; those default assignments are not confirmed information, and their number is shown in Data quality. Accounts without a business profile are counted separately. A category is corrected when the account holder picks a more suitable business type, describes the business and picks its sector, or asks for a catalog correction.

    Activation

    The locked activation definition cannot be computed by the server (the export step happens on the device), so no activation figure is shown.

    Definitions

    Operator-generated internal metrics. Aggregate counts only. Not a user-record attestation.
